Rooms without a point

Lower cost and shorter distance or transit time are better. A room is on a frontier when no other room in that comparison is at least as good on both measures and better on one. Dashed amber steps use the plotted values, including provisional rooms whose missing fees may raise their price. Steps mark a boundary, not additional offers. Hover or focus a point for its name.
